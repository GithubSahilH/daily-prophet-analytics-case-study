## 1. The business scenario
 
The Daily Prophet is a Sydney-based daily newspaper and digital publisher with two revenue streams:
 
- **Subscriptions**: four plans (Digital Basic, Digital Plus, Print + Digital, Weekend Print)
- **Advertising**: print, web, newsletter and sponsored content
I play the business analyst reporting to the CFO, supporting the Editor-in-Chief, Head of Subscriptions, Head of Advertising, Head of Technology and Chief Risk Officer.
 
**The core questions** (full list in the workbook's `Business_Questions` sheet):
 
| # | Stakeholder | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | CFO | Are we profitable each month, and which months are weakest? |
| Q2 | Head of Subscriptions | Which plans and acquisition channels churn fastest? |
| Q3 | Head of Subscriptions | What would an 8% Digital Basic price rise do to revenue if churn rises 2 points? |
| Q4 | Editor-in-Chief | Which sections drive new subscribers? |
| Q5 | Editor-in-Chief | Where is the bottleneck between pitch and publication? |
| Q6 | Head of Advertising | How concentrated is ad revenue, and how quickly do advertisers pay? |
| Q8 | Chief Risk Officer | Which high risks have weak or untested controls? |

## 5. The dataset
 
Run the generator to rebuild everything. Using the same seed gives identical data.
 
```bash
pip install pandas numpy openpyxl
python python/generate_daily_prophet.py --csv
```
 
This creates:
 
- `DailyProphet_Project.xlsx`: README, exercises, business questions and every data sheet
- `daily_prophet.db`: SQLite copy of the clean tables
- `csv/`: one CSV per clean table (for Tableau)
Options: `--seed 7`, `--subscribers 5000`, `--articles 2500`, `--campaigns 900`.
 
### Tables
 
| Table | Rows (default) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `subscribers` | 3,000 | Plan, price, channel, status, churn date and reason |
| `articles` | 1,500 | Section, author, paywall flag, pageviews, shares, subscribers attributed |
| `authors` | 30 | Section, staff or freelance, salary or per-article fee |
| `editorial_workflow` | 1,500 | Stage timestamps from pitch to publication, rework count |
| `advertisers` | 40 | Industry and account manager |
| `ad_campaigns` | 600 | Format, booked and invoiced amounts, payment timing and status |
| `monthly_costs` | 28 | Cost lines by month (revenue is derived by the analyst) |
| `risk_register` | 16 | Risks with likelihood, impact and controls |
 
**Data window:** 1 Jul 2024 to 31 Oct 2026, in AUD, on the Australian financial year (Jul to Jun).
 
### Deliberate data problems
 
The data includes problems for the analyst to find and handle:
 
- 8 articles credit an `AuthorID` that does not exist in `authors`
- A deliberately dirty legacy export (`Subscribers_Messy` sheet): mixed date formats, inconsistent plan and state names, currency symbols in numbers, duplicates and blank rows
- Overdue and written-off ad invoices

## 8. Assumptions and limitations
 
- All data is synthetic, so patterns reflect how the generator was written, not real market behaviour.
- **Churn** is defined as a subscriber with `Status = Churned`. Monthly churn rate = churned in month ÷ active at the start of the month.
- **MRR** is the sum of `MonthlyPrice` for active subscribers.
- **Ad revenue** is recognised in the month of `StartDate` for analysis purposes. A real business would apply its own recognition policy.
- **Scenario results** (price rise and churn effects) depend on the stated assumptions, not observed customer behaviour.
- Risk ratings and thresholds are my own judgement, justified in the risk paper.
- Regulatory references (for example APRA CPS 230 and CPS 234) are used as benchmarks only. They do not directly apply to a publisher.
